Somos SYALIA

Un startup cubano dedicado a la investigación y el desarrollo de productos y servicios basados en Inteligencia Artificial, Aprendizaje Automático, Ciencia de Datos, Analítica de Negocios, y más!

Nuestros servicios

Integra IA & ML en tu portafolio

Do you want to integrate AI & Machine Learning into your products? We can develop custom solutions based on state-of-the-art technology for you. Fine-tune pre-trained models o create models from scratch based on your data, for vision, text, audio, and virtually anything. You can deploy our models on-premise in your own infrastructure, or we can host them for you.

Usa nuestra plataforma de Inteligencia de Negocios

Subscribe to our custom BI platform, connect your data sources, and get access to smart tools for product recommendations, customer prediction, churn analysis, market insights, and more.

Organiza y entiende tus datos

Get the most out of your own data. We can help you build an organization-wide data lake for your own business, and build custom dashboards, analytic tools, and reports, to make your business data transparent and help you make better decisions.

Mantente al día con la IA

Surf above the hype, filter out the noise, and stay up-to-date with the latest trends in the AI industry. We can craft custom reports for your business domain and work with your team to get them up to speed with the best practices, standards, and tools used throughout your industry.
